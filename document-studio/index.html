Document Studio

Turn camera snapshots into crisp, flat, professionally enhanced digital scans. Perform 4-corner perspective warping, shadow removal, thresholding, and A4 print layout directly in your browser with selected offline capabilities.

Implemented Features

Everything Built Into Document Studio

Real, working capabilities engineered to give you scanner-quality documents right inside your browser.

📐

4-Corner Perspective Quad

Drag Top-Left, Top-Right, Bottom-Right, and Bottom-Left handles over paper edges. OpenCV computes homography matrix for flat rendering.

🎨

4 Filter Modes

Switch between B/W binary thresholding, smooth Grayscale, original Color preservation, and Unsharp Mask sharpening.

☁️

Shadow Removal Filter

Suppresses background gradients caused by room lighting, hands, or camera hardware, rendering an even white page background.

🎛️

4 Precision Sliders

Live tuning for Contrast (1.0 to 3.0), Brightness (-200 to 0), Saturation (0.0 to 3.0), and Sharpness (0.0 to 2.0).

🔄

Orientation & Zoom

Rotate document 0°, 90°, 180°, or 270°. Smooth mouse wheel zooming and click-and-drag panning on original and output canvases.

🖨️

A4 Sheet Print & PDF Batch

Direct A4 preview modal with scaling slider. Export single page image with custom name or compile all gallery pages into a multi-page PDF.

Step-by-Step Guide

How to Use Document Studio

Follow this 10-step guide to get clean, professional document scans from any camera photo.

1

Open Document Studio & Upload Your File

Click "Click or Drop Files" in the left sidebar or drag your image/PDF file directly onto the workspace. You can upload multiple files at once; they will appear in the right-hand Gallery roll.

💡 Tip: You can upload smartphone photos (JPG, PNG, WEBP) or existing PDF documents.
2

Align 4-Corner Perspective Anchors

Look at the "Original" canvas on the left. You will see 4 blue circular corner handles (TL, TR, BR, BL). Click and drag each handle to align with the four physical corners of your paper document. The cyan dotted polygon marks the active crop zone.

💡 Tip: Use your mouse wheel to zoom into corners for sub-pixel alignment precision.
3

Adjust Rotation if Necessary

If your photo was taken in landscape or upside down, use the Rotation dropdown in the sidebar to rotate by 90°, 180°, or 270°.

4

Select Your Desired Filter Mode

Choose from the 4 filter buttons: B/W (ideal for text documents and forms), Grayscale (recommended for certificates with stamps and photos), Color (keeps original tones), or Sharpen (adds edge contrast).

5

Toggle Shadow Removal

If parts of your page have harsh dark shadows caused by room lighting, click the "Shadows: Off" button to activate shadow suppression. The algorithm normalizes luminance across the paper.

6

Fine-Tune Contrast & Brightness

Use the Contrast slider to make dark text bolder and the Brightness slider to push grayish paper back to clean white.

7

Adjust Saturation & Sharpness

If keeping color, tweak Saturation. Adjust Sharpness to enhance character edge definition for higher optical clarity.

8

Inspect Enhanced Output Preview

Examine the "Enhanced Output" screen on the right. Any slider movement or corner repositioning updates the live render immediately.

9

Trigger A4 Sheet Print

Click "Print A4 Sheet". A modal will open displaying your enhanced document centered on a true A4 page (210 × 297 mm) with a scale offset slider. Click "Trigger System Print" to print.

10

Download Image or PDF

Click "Download Image" to open the filename modal, select your extension (JPG, PNG, WEBP, PDF), and save. Click "Download PDF" to compile all gallery pages into a clean, multi-page PDF.
