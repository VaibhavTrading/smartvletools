Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

Last Audited & Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

SMART VLE TOOL ("We", "Us", "Our", or "Platform") is committed to protecting your privacy, data security, and transparency. This Privacy Policy details how SMART VLE TOOL handles technical data, local browser storage, and third-party advertising services when you visit or use our online document, image, and PDF editing utilities.

2. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all pages, features, and studio tools hosted under SMART VLE TOOL, including Document Studio, ID Card Studio, and Image & PDF Editor.

3. Information We Collect

SMART VLE TOOL operates without mandatory user registration or account sign-in. Depending on how you interact with the Platform:

  • Technical Log Data: Standard HTTP request data logged by web hosting infrastructure, including IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, date/time stamps, and request paths.
  • Support Inquiries: Name, email address, subject, and message content voluntarily provided when contacting us via email or contact form.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

Factually and strictly:

  • We do NOT require user registration, passwords, or personal profiles.
  • We do NOT extract, store, or sell personal identity information contained within documents you edit.
  • We do NOT upload user original images, documents, or ID card files to a remote server for processing.

5. How We Use Information

Collected technical information is used strictly to:

  • Deliver website resources, scripts, and stylesheets efficiently.
  • Maintain platform operational stability, diagnostic logging, abuse protection, and security.
  • Deliver non-disruptive third-party advertisements via Google AdSense to keep our services free for all users.
  • Respond to user feedback, technical bug reports, and support inquiries.

6. Browser-Based Image & Document Processing

Image and document processing operations on SMART VLE TOOL take place directly inside your device's web browser using HTML5 Canvas, WebGL, OpenCV.js WebAssembly, and jsPDF:

  • Uploaded files remain entirely within your device memory.
  • Original files and processed outputs are not transmitted to our server during standard browser-based operations.
  • To prevent loss of ongoing work when switching tabs or refreshing, local canvas state and recent project thumbnails may be saved in your browser's local IndexedDB storage (Monu_DocIDStudio_DB and Monu_ImagePDFEditor_DB).

7. Local Browser Storage & Shared Device Notice

Projects and working data saved in your browser remain under your device control. If you use a shared or public device (such as a cyber café or school computer) to edit sensitive identity documents, please use the "Clear All Saved Data & Reset Studio" button above or clear your browser history/cache before closing your session.

8. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

SMART VLE TOOL is monetized through advertisements served by Google AdSense and its advertising partners:

  • Google uses cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to serve advertisements based on user browsing history.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.
  • Advertising networks do NOT receive access to your uploaded images, scanned documents, or processed files.
